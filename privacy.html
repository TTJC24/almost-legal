Privacy Policy — Almost

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Almost is a no-buy spending tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles — in short: it stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Almost does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics.

Data on your device

Everything you enter in the app — urge logs, streaks, money kept, cooling-room items, and settings — is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Almost Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see or handle your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

Almost is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children — because we do not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
