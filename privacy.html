Privacy Policy — Almost

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Almost is a no-buy spending tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles — in short: it stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Almost does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics.

Data on your device

Everything you enter in the app — urge logs, streaks, money kept, cooling-room items, and settings — is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Almost Plus subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see or handle your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Links to other services

Almost may show links to other services from the same publisher, such as Couch Cushions. Tapping one opens that website in your browser; Almost sends it nothing about you or your logs. That website's own privacy policy applies once you are there.

Partner (affiliate) links

Almost also shows a few clearly labelled partner links (for example, high-yield savings and budgeting services such as Raisin, Quontic, Crew, and YNAB). They are marked "Partner links. We may earn a commission." If you sign up with a partner after tapping one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

  • Partner links open in your browser and pass through a simple redirect at couchcushions.app, run by the same publisher, so we can update where a link points without an app update. The link carries only the name of the screen it was tapped on (for example, "settings_partners"). It contains no identifier and nothing about you, your logs, or your money kept.
  • We do not share your data with partners. Almost has no data to share: everything you enter stays on your device, and the app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Once you leave Almost, the partner (and any affiliate network that tracks the referral) may set its own cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy. We don't control that.
  • Partner cards can be dismissed, and that choice is stored only on your device.

Children

Almost is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children — because we do not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
